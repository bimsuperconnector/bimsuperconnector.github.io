# SuperConnector --- Terms & Privacy Requirements

Publish public `/terms` and `/privacy` pages and link them from the
public footer and onboarding/authentication surfaces.

## Terms of Service

Cover acceptance, eligibility/verified alumni, account responsibilities,
profile accuracy, acceptable use, professional conduct, user-generated
content, jobs/recruitment disclaimer, events, networking disclaimer, no
guarantee of employment/business outcomes, platform availability,
moderation/suspension, IP, third-party services, privacy reference,
liability, changes and contact details/effective date.

## Privacy Policy

Explain operator identity, data collected (Google identity,
profile/photo, education, employment, location, networking
participation/feedback, jobs/events and relevant technical/security
data), purposes, visibility/access by members/moderators/admins,
Google/Firebase/GitHub/Calendar/Meet processing, retention, security,
cookies/local storage/service worker, correction/deletion requests,
account removal, international processing where applicable, eligibility,
changes and contact/effective date.

Do not invent legal entity details, addresses, retention periods or
regulatory claims. Ask the owner for missing legal details and clearly
mark placeholders. These pages are product requirements, not legal
advice.

## Legal UI

Use the supplied design system: white canvas, readable content width,
strong hierarchy, generous spacing, mobile-friendly layout, effective
date, optional contents navigation, footer links.
